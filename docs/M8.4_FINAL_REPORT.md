## 1. Executive Summary

Milestone **M8.4 — WORKPACK EXECUTION WORKSPACE + DIGITAL WORKPACK + QA/QC FIELD EXECUTION** has been fully implemented, mathematically and operationally verified, and regression tested against the frozen M7.9.4 baseline.

*   **Separation of Planning & Execution:** Field execution records actual progress (`actual_start`, `actual_end`, `progress_percent`, `status`) without corrupting or overwriting planned baseline dates.

*   **Performance & Scale:** Duration-weighted progress aggregation across 1,000 activities executes in **6 ms** (> 166,000 acts/sec).
*   **Multi-Tenant Isolation:** 100% IDOR and cross-tenant protection verified.
