### CRITICAL DESIGN DECISION: Activity Family ≠ Execution Phase

**[RECOMMENDATION]** Activity Family and Execution Phase (Window) are TWO SEPARATE DIMENSIONS.

| Concept | Definition | Source | Used For |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Execution Phase** (Window) | WHEN the activity runs: PreSD, OP, SD, IR, PostSD | Section E "Window" column | Scheduling, progress reporting by phase |
| **Activity Family** | WHAT type of work: PREP, ISO, CLEAN, INSP, REPAIR... | Derived from activity description | Activity Library organization, template design |

> **[RECOMMENDATION]** These overlap but are NOT the same. Example: "De-blinding" is Activity Family=ISO but Execution Phase=SD or PostSD. "Inspection" is Activity Family=INSP but Execution Phase=IR. They correlate but do not map 1:1.
