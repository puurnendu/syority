# M8.6 Equipment Knowledge Versioning & Provenance

## 1. Versioning Philosophy
Equipment Knowledge facts can evolve as plant modifications occur or as higher-fidelity documents become available (e.g. As-Built Rev B replacing GA Rev A). We must not silently overwrite historical facts.

## 2. Versioning Mechanics

When a new extraction event occurs (either via AI or manual entry), a new `EquipmentKnowledgeVersion` record is created. 

- **Detection:** The system pulls the latest `VERIFIED` fields for the equipment from the `EquipmentKnowledgeMaster` state.
- **Comparison:** The newly extracted fields are diffed against the reference state.
- **Resolution:** The UI presents changes as `[Accept New]`, `[Keep Existing]`, or `[Edit]`.
- **Commit:** If changes are accepted, the old `VERIFIED` version transitions to `SUPERSEDED`, and the new version becomes `VERIFIED`.

This ensures that historical Workpacks referencing the older version of the equipment knowledge maintain their integrity, while new Workpacks automatically pick up the updated truths.

## 3. Field-Level Provenance

Accountability is paramount for industrial master data. Every `EquipmentKnowledgeField` inherently tracks its provenance:

1. **Source Traceability:** `source_document_id`, `source_page`, and `source_location` provide a direct click-through to the exact bounding box in the original PDF where the AI found the value.
2. **AI Metric:** `confidence_score` and `extraction_model_version` track *how* the data was obtained.
3. **Human Accountability:** `verified_by` and `verified_at` track *who* confirmed the data to be true.

By storing provenance at the field level rather than the version level, we allow a planner to verify that "Bundle Weight" came from the GA Drawing, while "Design Pressure" came from the P&ID.
