## 3. Milestone Goals (M8.5)

Establish the authoritative **Field Execution Control Center** for active shutdown operations:
1. **Daily Shift Workspace (`/execution`):** Shift lifecycle management (`Create` $\to$ `Open` $\to$ `Record` $\to$ `Close`).
2. **Deterministic Start/Finish & Progress Capture:** Strict isolation between Planning Baseline dates/CPM float and Execution actual dates/progress.
3. **Manpower Capture:** Real-time contractor, discipline, headcount, and manhours tracking.
4. **Delay & Constraint Logging:** Real-time delay classification without silent schedule mutations.
5. **Plan vs. Actual Analysis (`/execution/plan-vs-actual`):** Duration, start, and finish variance calculation.
6. **24-Hour & 72-Hour Lookahead Engine (`/execution/lookahead`):** Unified lookahead categorization (Starting, Continuing, Finishing, Overdue, Blocked, Critical).
7. **Shift Handover & Daily Execution Reports:** Derived operational views for seamless shift-to-shift continuity.
