# M8.4.1 — Workpack Execution Workspace Audit

Audit of the 10 core execution workspace dimensions on `/workpacks/[id]`.

---

## 1. Ten Core Execution Dimensions Audit

| # | Dimension | Implemented Component | API Route | Database Model | Classification | Evidence & Runtime Details |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Header / Workpack Cockpit** | `WorkpackLayout.tsx` | `/api/workpacks/[id]` | `Workpack`, `Organization`, `Site` | **IMPLEMENTED** | Displays Workpack Number, Title, Unit, Plant, System, Status badge, Revision, Overall Progress %, and Print/Action buttons. |
| **2** | **Scope of Work** | `OverviewTab` in `WorkpackTabs.tsx` | `/api/workpacks/[id]` | `Workpack.scope_of_work`, `Workpack.equipment_technical_data` | **IMPLEMENTED** | Displays description, equipment details, work type, priority, and date window. |
| **3** | **Activities Execution** | `ActivitiesPanel.tsx` | `/api/activities`, `/api/execution/activity-action` | `Activity`, `ProgressLog` | **IMPLEMENTED** | Lists all activities with duration, planned/actual dates, progress slider, and hold point badges. |
| **4** | **Materials Management** | `MaterialsTab.tsx` | `/api/workpacks/[id]/materials` | `workpack_material_lines`, `WorkpackMaterial` | **IMPLEMENTED** | Lists required materials, quantity required, issued, used, and returned. Supports operational tracking. |
| **5** | **Tools & Equipment** | `ToolsTab.tsx` | `/api/workpacks/[id]/tools` | `workpack_tools` | **IMPLEMENTED** | Lists specialized tools, quantity, status (Required/Available/Issued), and certification requirement flags. |
| **6** | **QA/QC Hold Points** | `QaClearanceTab.tsx` | `/api/workpacks/[id]/hold-points`, `/api/qa-clearance` | `qa_clearance_records`, `Activity.hold_point_type` | **IMPLEMENTED** | Displays mandatory hold points, inspection types, clearance certificates, witness signatures, and sign-off timestamps. |
| **7** | **Safety & JSA** | `AttachmentsTab.tsx` (JSA group) + `SafetyLog` | `/api/events/[eventId]/safety` | `SafetyLog`, `SafetyIncident`, `WorkpackAttachment` | **PARTIAL** | Event-level safety and workpack JSA attachments exist. Direct embedding of active shift safety logs in the workpack detail page is partial. |
| **8** | **Documents & Drawings** | `DocumentsTab.tsx` + `AttachmentsTab.tsx` | `/api/workpacks/[id]/documents` | `WorkpackDocument`, `WorkpackAttachment` | **IMPLEMENTED** | P&ID drawings, vendor manuals, procedures, and certificates attached and printable. |
| **9** | **Punch List Execution** | `PunchListTab` / `punch_list` | `/api/workpacks/[id]/punch-list` | `PunchListItem` / `punch_items` | **IMPLEMENTED** | Category A/B/C punch items with status (open/closed), photos, and closure validation gates. |
| **10**| **History & Audit Trail** | `WorkflowTimeline` + `AuditHistory` | `/api/workpacks/[id]/workflow` | `WorkflowTransition`, `AuditLog` | **IMPLEMENTED** | Complete chronological audit log of state changes, timestamps, users, and review comments. |

---

## 2. Workspace Assessment Summary

* **8 Dimensions IMPLEMENTED** (Cockpit, Scope, Activities, Materials, Tools, QA/QC, Documents, Punch List, History).
* **1 Dimension PARTIAL** (Direct Workpack-level Safety incident badge vs. Event-level Safety).
* **0 Dimensions PLACEHOLDER or BROKEN**.
