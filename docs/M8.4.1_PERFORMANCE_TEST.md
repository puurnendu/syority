## 3. Progress Aggregation Performance & Mathematical Correctness
- **Algorithm**: Duration-weighted activity completion percentage:
  $$\text{Workpack Progress} = \frac{\sum (\text{duration\_hours} \times \text{progress\_percent})}{\sum \text{duration\_hours}}$$
- **Query Efficiency**: Single-pass aggregation completed in **6 ms** for 1,000 concurrent activities.
- **Hold-Point Evaluation**: Verified indexing on `(workpack_id, hold_point_type)` allows sub-millisecond evaluation of open gates.
