## 2. Detailed Priority Breakdown

### P0 — Must Exist for First Commercial Release (The Core Baseline)
These features represent the minimum viable product (MVP) that allows an industrial turnaround team or contractor to manage a shutdown from start to finish without paper spreadsheets:

1.  **3-Minute Local Setup Wizard:** One-time initial setup creating Company Name, Site Name, and Admin credentials.
2.  **Top-Bar Active Shutdown Switcher:** A persistent dropdown allowing planners and managers to toggle between active/historical shutdowns (`TA-2026`, `TA-2028`), ensuring all downstream screens immediately scope to that shutdown.
3.  **Spreadsheet-Style WBS & Activity Editor:** Rapid keyboard-navigable grid for adding and editing 500+ shutdown activities with disciplines, durations, and contractor assignments.
4.  **Interactive Gantt Chart & CPM Engine:** Visual timeline showing predecessor/successor ties, total float, and driving critical path.
5.  **Workpack Generation & Assembly:** Atomic sequential workpack numbering (`U10-MEC-001`), material/joint assignments, and 1-click merged PDF workpack binder printing.
6.  **Flange Joint & Blind Register:** Flange torque values, gasket material lookup tables (ASME/PCC-1), and blind install/removal tracking.
7.  **QA/QC Hold Point Clearances:** Inspection sign-offs, box-up clearance milestones, and punch list resolution.
8.  **Daily Safety (HSE) Logging:** Daily manhours, PTW counts, toolbox talks, and incident logging with LTI tracking.
9.  **24h / 72h Lookahead & Shift Handover Reports:** Standardized shift handover reports and lookahead task schedules for morning coordination meetings.
10. **Executive 30-Second Situation Dashboard:** High-contrast cockpit answering: *Are we on schedule? What is on the critical path? How many workpacks are ready? What constraints are blocking us today?*

---

### P1 — Important Turnaround Enhancers (Syority AI & File Interop)
These capabilities provide dramatic time savings and form the monetization core:

1.  **AI Workpack Procedural Auto-Fill:** 1-click generation of step-by-step disassembly/inspection/boxup procedures, safety hazards, and tool checklists.
2.  **Multimodal P&ID Drawing Data Extraction:** Uploading scanned P&ID drawings to automatically extract valve tags, line numbers, and piping specifications into scope items.
3.  **Field Audio Memo Progress Transcriber:** Field supervisors record a 30-second voice memo on their phone (*"Pump 1001 impeller removed, shaft shows pitting, 50% complete"*), which AI converts into structured shift progress logs.
4.  **Primavera P6 & MS Project File I/O:** Bi-directional import and export of native `.XER` and `.XML` schedule files.
5.  **Touch-Friendly Mobile/Tablet Field View (`/field`):** High-contrast responsive interface optimized for field supervisors logging activity progress on mobile devices under bright sunlight.

---

### P2 — Useful Later (Ecosystem Integrations)
1.  **Bidirectional SAP PM Order Sync:** Direct synchronization with SAP Plant Maintenance work orders via standard RFC/BAPI connectors.
2.  **WhatsApp Shift Summary Dispatcher:** Automatic dispatch of 06:00 AM executive progress summaries to department managers via WhatsApp Business API.
3.  **Historical Lesson-Learned AI Search:** Semantic retrieval across previous shutdowns (*"What caused the delay on FCC reactor head removal in TA-2022?"*).

---

### P3 — Enterprise & Multi-Site Portfolio
1.  **Multi-Site Corporate Rollup:** Corporate dashboard aggregating health across multiple regional refineries/plants.
2.  **Enterprise Single Sign-On (SSO):** SAML 2.0 / Azure AD / Okta corporate directory federation.
3.  **White-Label Corporate Branding:** Custom logo, themes, and certificate letterheads per corporate enterprise client.
