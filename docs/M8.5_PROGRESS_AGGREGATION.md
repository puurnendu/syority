# M8.5 — Progress Aggregation Engine & Progress Model

This document defines the mathematical aggregation method for calculating **Activity Progress**, **Workpack Progress**, and **Shutdown Level Progress** in SYORITY.

---

## 1. Mathematical Progress Model

### A. Activity Progress ($P_i$)
* Discrete or continuous milestone completion: $0\%, 25\%, 50\%, 75\%, 100\%$.
* Status mapping:
  * $P_i = 0\% \iff \text{status} = \text{not\_started}$
  * $0\% < P_i < 100\% \iff \text{status} = \text{in\_progress}$
  * $P_i = 100\% \iff \text{status} = \text{completed}$

### B. Workpack Overall Progress ($P_{\text{WP}}$)
Duration-weighted average by activity planned duration hours ($D_i$):
$$P_{\text{WP}} = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n} (P_i \times D_i)}{\sum_{i=1}^{n} D_i}$$
*(If all activities have zero planned hours, simple unweighted arithmetic mean is used).*

### C. Shutdown Level Progress ($P_{\text{Shutdown}}$)
Aggregated across all Workpacks and Standalone Activities belonging to the active turnaround campaign (`event_id`):
$$P_{\text{Shutdown}} = \frac{\sum_{\text{activities}} (P_i \times D_i)}{\sum_{\text{activities}} D_i}$$

---

## 2. Invariant Rules

1. Field execution updates `actual_start`, `actual_end`, `progress_percent`, and `status`.
2. Field execution **NEVER** modifies `planned_start`, `planned_end`, Early/Late dates, or CPM float values.
3. Progress percentage strictly adheres to $0 \le P_i \le 100$.
