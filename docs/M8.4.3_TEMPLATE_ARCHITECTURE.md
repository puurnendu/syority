## Template Hierarchy Investigation

### Is Equipment Type = Template?

**[FACT]** NO. A Heat Exchanger can have at least 2 distinct scope profiles:

1. **HE Simple** (WP 9000009485): 56 activities — gasket replacement, cleaning, inspection
2. **HE Complex** (WP 9000009501): 105 activities — full retubing with tube pulling, insertion, expansion, multiple QA hold points

### Correct Model: Equipment Type × Job Type = Template

**[RECOMMENDATION]** The template dimension is Equipment Type × Job Type (or Work Type).
