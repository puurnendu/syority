## Current TA Dashboard Structure

**File**: `app/(dashboard)/projects/[id]/ta-dashboard/page.tsx` (1300+ lines)
**Duplicate**: `app/(dashboard)/events/[eventId]/ta-dashboard/page.tsx` (same component)

### Current Tabs
1. **Overview** — S-curve, KPI cards, unit progress, lookahead, AI jobs, safety
2. **Progress** — Progress ring, duration analysis, activity status, identical activities, equipment/discipline/contractor/workpack tables, equipment drill-down, critical/lagging
3. **Lookahead** — Today's activities
4. **Constraints** — Constraint list with severity
5. **Punch** — Punch list items with A/B/C categories
6. **Resources** — Resource histogram
7. **Report** — AI-generated daily progress report

## Verdict: TA Dashboard Should Be the PRIMARY TA Command Centre

The current TA Dashboard is architecturally correct as a **command centre** that aggregates all module intelligence. It should NOT be split into separate module dashboards.

### Recommended Information Architecture

```
TA COMMAND CENTRE (ta-dashboard)
├── Tab: OVERVIEW
│   ├── Overall Progress Ring (authoritative)
│   ├── S-Curve (planned vs actual)
│   ├── KPI Cards: SPI, CPI, Days Remaining, Progress %
│   ├── Today's Critical Jobs (AI-assisted)
│   ├── Safety Summary
│   └── Decision Queue (new)
│
├── Tab: PROGRESS INTELLIGENCE
│   ├── Authoritative Progress Metrics
│   ├── Identical Activity Intelligence
│   ├── Equipment Drill-Down (expand/collapse)
│   ├── Discipline / Contractor / Workpack Tables
│   ├── Critical / Lagging Activities
│   └── Filters: Equipment Type, Discipline, Contractor
│
├── Tab: SCHEDULE
│   ├── Milestone Tracker
│   ├── Critical Path Summary
│   ├── Float Analysis
│   └── Schedule Variance
│
├── Tab: EVM (new)
│   ├── SPI / CPI Cards
│   ├── Earned Value Curve
│   ├── Variance Analysis
│   └── Forecast (EAC, ETC)
│
├── Tab: RESOURCES
│   ├── Resource Histogram
│   ├── Resource Utilization
│   └── Overtime / Undertime
│
├── Tab: LOOKAHEAD
│   ├── Day / Week / 2-Week views
│   ├── Readiness check per activity
│   └── Material/constraint blocking
│
├── Tab: CONSTRAINTS
│   ├── Open constraints by severity
│   ├── Aging analysis
│   └── Owner assignment
│
├── Tab: MATERIALS
│   ├── Material readiness summary
│   ├── Outstanding items
│   └── Impact on schedule
│
├── Tab: SAFETY
│   ├── LTI / Near Miss / First Aid
│   ├── PTW Summary
│   ├── Manhours Safety Statistics
│   └── Incident Log
│
├── Tab: SCOPE / DISCOVERY
│   ├── Scope changes (approved/pending)
│   ├── Discovery work items
│   └── Impact on progress
│
├── Tab: PUNCH
│   ├── Punch by category (A/B/C)
│   ├── Aging analysis
│   └── Completion trend
│
└── Tab: REPORT
    ├── AI Daily Progress Report
    ├── Management Report
    └── Export to PDF
```
