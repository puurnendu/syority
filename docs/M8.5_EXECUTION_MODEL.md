## 2. Core Execution Entity Specifications

### A. Shift Management Contract
* **Identification:** Unique combination of `(event_id, log_date, shift)`.
* **Shifts Supported:** Standard industrial `day` (07:00 – 19:00) and `night` (19:00 – 07:00) rotations.
* **Lifecycle States:** `open` $\to$ `active_execution` $\to$ `handover_ready` $\to$ `closed`.
* **Constraint:** No duplicate active shift logs permitted for the same shutdown date and shift rotation.

### B. Activity Execution & Progress Contract
* **Separation Invariant:**
  * **Planning Baseline:** `planned_start`, `planned_end`, `duration_hours`, CPM Early/Late dates, Total Float, Critical Path.
  * **Execution Actuals:** `actual_start`, `actual_end`, `progress_percent`, `status`, `actual_duration`, `remaining_duration`.
* **State Progression Rules:**
  * `progress_percent = 0%` $\iff$ `status = not_started`
  * `0% < progress_percent < 100%` $\iff$ `status = in_progress`
  * `progress_percent = 100%` $\iff$ `status = completed`

### C. Manpower & Headcount Model
* **Dimensions:** Shift, Contractor, Discipline, Workpack, Headcount, Manhours.
* **Auto-Calculation:** $\text{Manhours} = \text{Headcount} \times \text{Shift Duration (e.g., 10 or 12h)}$.
* **Supervisor Override:** Permitted with justification notes stored in the audit record.

### D. Delays & Constraints Model
* **Classification Categories:** `MATERIAL`, `PERMIT`, `SCAFFOLD`, `ACCESS`, `VENDOR`, `DOCUMENT`, `MANPOWER`, `EQUIPMENT`, `WEATHER`, `INSPECTION`, `OTHER`.
* **Severity Levels:** `critical`, `high`, `medium`, `low`.
* **Schedule Decoupling:** Logging a field delay records an operational impediment and affects execution variance, but **NEVER** mutates baseline CPM predecessor/successor links or planned baseline dates.

### E. Lookahead & Plan vs. Actual Engine
* **24-Hour & 72-Hour Lookahead:** Categorizes activities into:
  1. `STARTING`: Planned start within lookahead window and not yet started.
  2. `CONTINUING`: Started prior, currently in progress.
  3. `FINISHING`: Planned finish within lookahead window.
  4. `OVERDUE`: Planned finish in the past with progress < 100%.
  5. `BLOCKED`: Has open critical/high constraints or uncleared hold points.
  6. `CRITICAL`: Activity is on the driving critical path (`is_critical = true` or `total_float <= 0`).
* **Variance Computation:**
  * $\text{Start Variance} = \text{Actual Start} - \text{Planned Start}$
  * $\text{Finish Variance} = \text{Actual Finish} - \text{Planned Finish}$
