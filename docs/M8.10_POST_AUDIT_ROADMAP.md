# Post-M8.9 Product Capability Audit & M8.10+ Roadmap

## 1. Executive Summary
Following the successful certification of M8.7 (Resource Leveling), M8.8 (Schedule Intelligence), and M8.9 (Scenario Planning), the STO (Shutdown, Turnaround, Outage) platform possesses a formidable core scheduling engine. An enterprise turnaround planner can now execute a closed-loop schedule control process: baselining, tracking progress, analyzing variances, running what-if scenarios, and promoting approved changes. 

However, to achieve total enterprise dominance against legacy tools (e.g., Primavera P6, MS Project) and specialized turnaround software, the platform must evolve from **Schedule & Resource Intelligence** to **Cost, Scope, and Probabilistic Intelligence**.

## 3. Gap Analysis

### 3.1 Data Model & Analytical Gaps
- **Earned Value Management (EVM):** The system tracks `budgeted_cost` and `actual_cost`, but lacks the temporal data structures for Planned Value (PV) and Earned Value (EV) curves (S-Curves). Cost Performance Index (CPI) cannot be accurately calculated.
- **Scope Change Control:** M8.9 Change Requests mutate activity dates/durations. Enterprise turnarounds experience massive *Scope Growth* (discovery work). There is no workflow to propose, price, approve, and baseline *new* activities mid-execution.
- **Probabilistic Risk:** The deterministic CPM engine (M8.8) yields a single finish date. Real-world turnarounds require 3-point estimates (Optimistic, Nominal, Pessimistic) to calculate P50 / P90 confidence intervals.

### 3.2 Integration & UX Gaps
- **Supply Chain Integration:** Material constraints are logged, but material ETA dates are not dynamically driving Activity `early_start` dates in the CPM engine.
- **P6 Co-Existence:** While imports exist, enterprise clients often demand a bi-directional XER/XML sync where P6 remains the system of record for finance, but STO is the system of execution.

## 4. M8.10+ Prioritized Roadmap

*Note: All certified M8.7, M8.8, and M8.9 modules are structurally protected. New features will be implemented as additive domain services.*

### 🟥 P0: Critical Enterprise Capabilities (M8.10 - M8.11)
These features are mandatory blockers for Tier-1 enterprise adoption where financial tracking and scope creep are board-level concerns.

1. **M8.10: Enterprise EVM & Cost Intelligence**
   - **Deliverable:** Cost-loaded schedules, PV/EV/AC S-Curves, CPI and TCPI forecasting.
   - **Value:** Upgrades the platform from a "Time" manager to a "Time & Money" manager.
2. **M8.11: Scope Change & Discovery Work Workflow**
   - **Deliverable:** Extend M8.9 `ScheduleChangeRequest` to `ScopeChangeRequest`. Allow creation of new Workpacks/Activities in a Sandboxed Scenario, pricing them, and approving them into the live Baseline.
   - **Value:** Captures the 20-30% scope growth typical in turnarounds, preventing unbilled work.

### 🟨 P1: Advanced Analytics & Optimization (M8.12 - M8.14)
These features differentiate the platform from legacy competitors by leveraging modern compute power.

3. **M8.12: Material & Supply Chain CPM Integration**
   - **Deliverable:** Hard-link SAP/ERP material ETA dates to Activity Logic as virtual predecessors. If a valve is delayed, the CPM automatically calculates the impact on the turnaround end-date.
4. **M8.13: Monte Carlo Schedule Risk Analysis (SRA)**
   - **Deliverable:** Allow 3-point duration estimates. Run 1,000+ simulation passes (leveraging the fast M8.8 `scheduleEngine`) to generate a probability distribution bell curve for the Event Finish Date.
5. **M8.14: AI-Driven Auto-Optimization**
   - **Deliverable:** Evolve M8.9 from "Manual Scenarios" to "Generative Scenarios". Allow the planner to ask: *"AI, generate a scenario that resolves the crane constraint without delaying the critical path."*

### 🟩 P2: Ecosystem & Execution (M8.15+)
6. **M8.15: P6 / MS Project Bi-Directional Sync Hub**
   - **Deliverable:** Deep XER/XML parsing, maintaining calendar IDs, advanced logic types, and WBS hierarchies for seamless round-tripping.
7. **M8.16: Field Execution Mobile App**
   - **Deliverable:** Tablet/Phone interface for foremen to scan barcodes, log shift progress, and flag constraints in real-time, feeding the `ProgressLog` directly.
