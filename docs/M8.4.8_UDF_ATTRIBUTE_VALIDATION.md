## Key Design Decisions

1. **QUANTITY attributes** have both Planned and Actual — tracked in ActivityUdfValue and ProgressLog respectively
2. **ENUM attributes** (Phase, Discipline, Permit) are stored as native Activity fields, not UDFs
3. **DERIVED attributes** (Critical Path) are computed by the schedule engine, never entered
4. **QA attributes** (Hold Stage, Witness Stage) control inspection workflow
5. **EXECUTION attributes** (Permit, Rigging, Crew Size) are captured during field execution
