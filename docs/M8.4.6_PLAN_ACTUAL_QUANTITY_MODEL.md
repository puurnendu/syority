## Architecture

```
PLANNED: ActivityUdfValue.value_number (planner enters at workpack creation)
                    ↓
ACTUAL:  ProgressLog.quantity_actual (field supervisor enters daily/shift)
                    ↓
REMAINING: Planned - Actual (calculated)
                    ↓
PROGRESS: Actual / Planned × 100 (calculated)
```
