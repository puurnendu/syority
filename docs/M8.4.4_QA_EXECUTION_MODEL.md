## The Two QA Archetypes
**[FACT]** Section G/H analysis confirms two distinct QA types.

| Type | Frequency | Behavior | Duration | SYORITY Mapping |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Hold Point** | ~90% | Blocking gate. Work stops until cleared. | 1-240 hrs | `hold_point_type = "HOLD"` |
| **Witness Point** | ~10% | Advisory. Inspector notified, work proceeds. | 0-8 hrs | `hold_point_type = "WITNESS"` |

## QA vs Progress Distortion
**[FACT]** A 2-hour QA inspection should not carry the same progress weight as a 240-hour retubing activity.

**[RECOMMENDATION]** RECOMMENDATION: Use Duration-Weighted Progress for physical execution. Track QA Clearance separately as a distinct KPI (e.g. "45/50 Hold Points Cleared").
