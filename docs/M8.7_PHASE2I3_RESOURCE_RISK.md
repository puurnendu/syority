# M8.7 Phase 2I.3 - Resource Risk & Early Warning Engine

## Implementation Details
The `ResourceRiskService` aggregates the KPI and Forecast data with the CPM baseline float to determine actionable risk scores.

## Risk Heuristic
The score is composed of:
1. `utilization_risk`: Up to 50 points
2. `deficit_risk`: Based on magnitude of headcount/quantity deficit
3. `float_risk`: Escalates rapidly if near-critical activities (< 2 days float) are affected
4. `critical_activity_risk`: Massive penalty (100+) if critical path is affected
5. `consecutive_shortage_risk`: Penalty based on consecutive days of failure
6. `multi_resource_risk`: Scaling penalty for days containing multiple distinct resource conflicts.

Risks are exported with explicit, human-readable reasons and actionable recommendations.
